Activity 2: Use Safely & Ethically

Navigate real-world scenarios and learn what to do—and what never to do.

You'll see three real-world scenarios. For each one, choose an option and see the consequence. There are no "wrong" answers—just learn from each choice.

📝 Scenario 1: Writing Your Essay
Your history essay is due tomorrow. You're stuck on how to start it. You think about using ChatGPT to write the whole thing for you.
🔐 Scenario 2: Sharing Information
You're testing an AI chatbot and you paste your school schedule, your friend's phone number, and your home address so it can "help" you plan your week.
👥 Scenario 3: Deepfakes & Impersonation
You discover an AI tool that can create fake videos of celebrities. Your friends think it's funny. You're tempted to try it.
