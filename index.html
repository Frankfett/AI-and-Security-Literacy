Your Journey to Digital Literacy

AI is everywhere. The internet connects everything. And with that power comes responsibility—and risk.

This learning path teaches you to understand how AI works, protect yourself online, and validate what you see before you trust it.

You'll complete five activities that build on each other. Some teach concepts. Some give you hands-on practice with real AI. By the end, you'll have the critical thinking skills to navigate the digital world safely and ethically.
