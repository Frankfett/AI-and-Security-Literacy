Verify Before You Trust

AI can make mistakes, hallucinate facts, or be outdated. Always verify what it tells you. Here's how to spot red flags.

AI Claim
"The Great Wall of China is 50,000 miles long and was built in just 10 years using only shovels and picks."
🚩 Spot the Red Flags:
📚 How to Verify:
Step 1: Google the claim yourself.
Step 2: Check reliable sources (Wikipedia, National Geographic, government websites, academic papers).
Step 3: See if multiple independent sources agree.
Step 4: If sources disagree, read carefully to understand why.

The Real Facts: The Great Wall is actually about 13,000 miles long (depending on what you measure) and took over 2,000 years to build, with many different dynasties contributing.
🎯 Your Turn:
Next time an AI (or anyone) tells you something important, ask yourself:
Does this sound too extreme or too simple?
Can I find the same information from other trusted sources?
Is there a date on this information? Could it be outdated?
Do I understand where the information came from?
