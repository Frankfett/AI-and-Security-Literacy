Activity 3: Verify Before You Trust

AI makes mistakes. Learn to spot red flags and fact-check before trusting claims.

Here's a claim that an AI might make. Let's analyze it together to spot red flags and learn how to verify it.

AI Claim
"The Great Wall of China is 50,000 miles long and was built in just 10 years using only shovels and picks."
🚩 Spot the Red Flags:
📚 How to Verify Any Claim:
Step 1: Google the claim yourself. Search for "Great Wall of China length" and see what trusted sources say.
Step 2: Check reliable sources. Wikipedia, National Geographic, government websites, and academic papers are usually trustworthy.
Step 3: See if multiple sources agree. If 5 sources say "13,000 miles" but AI said "50,000 miles," the sources win.
Step 4: If sources disagree, read carefully. Understand why they might be different (different definitions, updates over time, etc.).
🎯 Next Time You Hear a Claim (from AI or anyone):
Does this sound too extreme or too simple?
Can I find the same info from other trusted sources?
Is there a date on this info? Could it be outdated?
Do I understand where the information came from?
